ABES ENGINEERING COLLEGE - KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT ABES

ABES Engineering College is located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.
The official ABES website states that the institution was established in 2000.
The college campus is located at 19th KM Stone, NH-09, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, PIN 201009.

ABES states that it has been an autonomous college since 2025.

COURSES AND PROGRAMS

ABES offers programs including B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, MBA and BCA.

B.Tech branches listed by ABES include:
- Computer Science and Engineering
- Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Computer Science and Engineering with Data Science
- Electronics and Communication Engineering
- Electrical and Computer Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering

CSE INFORMATION

The Computer Science and Engineering department was established in 2000.

The B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering program is a four-year full-time program.

The department provides laboratories, project facilities, advanced labs and Centers of Excellence.

The department also conducts training, workshops, guest lectures, alumni lectures and project-based activities.

ACADEMICS AND TRAINING

ABES provides technical and soft-skills training for students.

Training programs are intended to improve technical skills, problem-solving ability, industry readiness and employability.

Training areas mentioned on the official website include Full Stack Development, DSA, DBMS, Data Science and company-readiness programs.

LIBRARY

The ABES library is described as a resource center supporting academic learning and research.

The official website states that the library has a large collection of books, e-journals and e-books and provides reading facilities.

The library is automated and uses the KOHA library management system.

IMPORTANT RULE FOR CHATBOT

The chatbot should answer using information available in this knowledge base.
